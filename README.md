# NBA Player Selection Analysis

## Overview

This project examines how college affiliation, draft year, physical characteristics, and player origin relate to NBA draft position.

**Research Question:** How are college affiliation, non-college or international pathways, and draft year associated with draft position after accounting for height, weight, and country of origin?

## Methods

We analyzed historical NBA player data to examine relationships between draft position and player characteristics. The analysis used regression modeling to estimate how college affiliation, draft year, height, weight, and country of origin are associated with draft position.

## Tools and Technologies

* R 
* tidyverse 
* ggplot2 
* Regression analysis

## Outcomes

The analysis evaluates whether readily available background and physical characteristics can help explain differences in NBA draft position. The results provide insight into historical patterns in player selection and whether these characteristics could be useful during the early stages of the scouting process.

## What I Learned

This project strengthened my experience with regression modeling and interpreting relationships between multiple variables. I also learned how to account for potential confounding factors when examining relationships in observational data and how to communicate statistical findings in the context of a real-world decision-making problem.
